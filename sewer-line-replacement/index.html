Sewer Line Replacement

Sewer Line Replacement in Pasadena, TX

When a sewer line has failed in multiple spots, has collapsed sections, or is old clay pipe that's deteriorated beyond what a spot repair or lining can fix, full lateral replacement is the honest answer -- and we'll show you the camera footage that gets us there, not just tell you it's needed.

We handle both traditional open-trench replacement and trenchless pipe bursting, which pulls a new line through the path of the old one with far less yard disruption where soil and access allow it.

Every replacement includes the post-work camera pass and the permit sign-off as part of the job, not a separate line item you have to ask for.

Signs You Need This

When To Call For Sewer Line Replacement

  • ✓Camera inspection shows collapsed or heavily deteriorated sections
  • ✓Repeated backups even after a repair or line clearing
  • ✓The line is original clay or Orangeburg pipe reaching end of life
  • ✓Multiple damaged sections found along the same lateral
  • ✓A camera inspection already on file showing a collapsed or bellied section
In Pasadena, TX

A meaningful share of Pasadena's housing stock dates to the mid-20th century, old enough that some original sewer laterals are still clay or Orangeburg pipe -- both materials that eventually deteriorate structurally regardless of how well the rest of the plumbing has been maintained.

That age profile is also why we see full lateral replacement more often in Pasadena's original neighborhoods than in the newer developments further from the historic core, where PVC lines installed from the start simply haven't reached the same point of material fatigue.

Not every damaged line needs this: a localized crack is often better addressed with a targeted spot repair instead. We'll also check whether the same clay-soil movement is affecting your water service line while we're already assessing the property.

Replacing a full lateral is a bigger project near Pasadena's bayous, where clay stays saturated after rain. For yards near Vince Bayou and Armand Bayou we look at trenchless options first whenever the condition of the pipe allows it.
