Water Heater Repair

Water Heater Repair in Pasadena, TX

A water heater that's leaking, making popping or rumbling noises, or putting out lukewarm water instead of hot is usually telling you something specific: a failing thermocouple or igniter on a gas unit, a burned-out heating element on an electric one, or sediment that's built up on the tank floor and is now insulating the burner from the water above it.

We diagnose the actual failure before recommending a fix. Most repairs (a new thermocouple, a replacement heating element, a flush to clear sediment, a new dip tube), are completed the same day, and we'll tell you plainly when a tank is old enough that replacement is the better value than another repair.

Either way, you'll know the actual cause before you're asked to approve anything, and we won't recommend replacement just because a unit has a few years on it.

Signs You Need This

When To Call For Water Heater Repair

  • ✓Water takes noticeably longer to heat up, or runs out faster than it used to
  • ✓Popping, rumbling, or crackling sounds from the tank
  • ✓Rusty or discolored water coming from the hot tap only
  • ✓Water pooling at the base of the tank or on the floor nearby
  • ✓The pilot light or igniter clicking repeatedly without the burner catching
In Pasadena, TX

Mineral sediment buildup is one of the most common reasons a water heater calls for repair years before its rated lifespan is up. A flush every 12 months keeps that buildup from insulating the burner and shortening the tank's life.

Gulf Coast summer heat also means a water heater here runs through more temperature swings between the incoming groundwater temperature and household demand than in milder climates, which is one more reason a unit that isn't flushed regularly tends to show wear earlier in Pasadena than in a drier, cooler market.

If the unit turns out to be past the point where repair makes sense, we'll walk you through replacement options on the same visit. Homes in Deer Park and similar-era neighborhoods often face this exact call around the same age.

Water heater calls come in from every part of the city, from the older blocks near Pasadena High School on South Shaver to the neighborhoods around Golden Acres Park and the Genoa Red Bluff corridor in the south. Wherever the unit sits, we flush it, test it, and tell you honestly whether it is worth repairing.

24/7 Emergency Response

Emergency Water Heater Leak or No Hot Water in Pasadena, TX

A water heater that is leaking onto the floor or has gone cold in the middle of the day is one of the most common emergencies we dispatch. Our 24/7 line sends a licensed technician who can tell on the spot whether the problem is a repair or a replacement.

If water is coming from the tank itself, shutting things off quickly limits the damage.

  • ✓Close the cold water supply valve on the pipe above the heater
  • ✓Turn the power off at the breaker for an electric unit, or set a gas unit to pilot or off
  • ✓Do not touch the hot discharge pipe or the water pooled around the unit
  • ✓If water keeps spreading, shut the main valve to the house, then call our 24/7 line
