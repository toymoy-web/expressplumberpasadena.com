Terms of Service

Last updated 2026

These terms govern your use of this website and your engagement of Pasadena Express Plumbing for plumbing services in Pasadena, TX and the surrounding area.

Services

Pasadena Express Plumbing provides licensed residential and light commercial plumbing repair, installation, and emergency service. A flat price is provided and must be approved by you before any billable work begins. Final scope may be adjusted if the technician discovers a materially different issue once work is underway, in which case we'll explain the change and get your approval before proceeding further.

Standard appointments are scheduled by phone. If you need to reschedule or cancel a booked visit, please call as far in advance as possible so we can offer that time to another customer.

Payment is due upon completion of work unless another arrangement has been agreed to in advance. The price quoted before work begins is the price you'll be charged, absent a scope change you've separately approved.

All plumbing work is performed by technicians licensed through the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners. Permits are pulled for any work that requires one under applicable code.
