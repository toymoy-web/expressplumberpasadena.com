Backflow Testing & Prevention

Backflow Testing & Prevention in Pasadena, TX

A backflow preventer keeps contaminated water from siphoning back into your clean water supply: required on irrigation systems and many commercial properties, and it needs annual testing to stay certified.

We test, certify, repair, and install backflow prevention devices, and file the required paperwork with the water utility so your certification stays current without you having to chase it down.

If your property's specific setup calls for a different device type than what's currently installed, we'll flag that during testing rather than simply pass or fail what's there.

Signs You Need This

When To Call For Backflow Testing & Prevention

  • ✓Your annual backflow certification is due or has lapsed
  • ✓A new irrigation system or commercial fixture needs a device installed
  • ✓The device is visibly leaking or failing a self-test
  • ✓You've received a notice from the water utility about testing
  • ✓A backflow device that's visibly discharging water from its relief vent
  • ✓A new irrigation system installed without a device already in place
In Pasadena, TX

Properties with irrigation systems anywhere in the Pasadena water utility's service area are required to have a tested, certified backflow preventer, and the utility does follow up on lapsed certifications. We handle the testing and the paperwork filing together so nothing falls through.

Commercial properties along the Ship Channel industrial corridor often have additional backflow requirements tied to their specific water use, which is worth confirming separately from the standard residential irrigation requirement most homeowners are familiar with.

Commercial properties should also see our commercial plumbing page for broader coverage, and homes with hard-water concerns alongside backflow testing often ask about softener installation during the same visit.

Businesses around the Pasadena Convention Center and Fairgrounds and along the Fairmont Parkway restaurant strip often need annual backflow testing to stay compliant. We test the assembly, report the result, and repair it if it fails.

Our Process

How We Handle It

1

Test the device

A certified test checks the assembly's check valves and relief mechanism.

2

Repair if it fails

A failed component is replaced, then the device is retested.

3

File certification

The passing test result is filed with the water utility on your behalf.
