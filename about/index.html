About Pasadena Express Plumbing

Local, licensed plumbing repair and installation for Pasadena, TX and the surrounding Houston metro area.

What We Do

Pasadena Express Plumbing handles residential and light commercial plumbing across Pasadena, Texas: repair, installation, and emergency service for everything from a running toilet to a full whole-home repipe. We work throughout Pasadena's older neighborhoods near the historic core and the newer construction further out, along with the nearby communities listed on our service area page.

Every technician we send is licensed through the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners and insured. We quote a flat price before any work begins, and we tell you honestly when a repair makes more sense than a replacement, or the other way around.

Why We Focus On Pasadena Specifically

Pasadena has a genuinely mixed housing stock: homes near the historic downtown core that still run original 1940s-1970s plumbing, newer construction further out toward the Harris County line, and everything in between. That range matters, because the right fix for a 1950s house with galvanized supply lines is often a completely different job than the right fix for a home built in the last two decades. We don't assume one answer fits every address in this city, and we size every recommendation to the specific home in front of us.

Being based in Pasadena also means we're familiar with the conditions that shape plumbing here specifically: the area's genuinely hard municipal water, the Gulf Coast clay soil that stresses foundations and the lines running beneath them, the occasional hard freeze that catches homes built for a mild climate off guard, and the older cast iron and galvanized pipe still common in the city's original neighborhoods. That local knowledge shapes how we diagnose a problem, not just how we fix it, and it's part of why our technicians can usually tell you what's likely going on before they've even opened a wall.

What You Can Expect On A Call

Every call starts the same way, whether it's a routine repair or a 2am emergency: we ask what's happening, get a technician dispatched, and give you a flat price before any billable work begins. There's no separate charge just to look at the problem, and no guessing after the fact. You approve the number, then we fix it.

If a repair genuinely makes more sense than a replacement, or the other way around, we'll say so plainly and explain why, rather than defaulting to whichever option costs more. That's true whether we're talking about a single water heater repair or a decision as significant as a whole-home repipe. We'd rather you trust the recommendation than feel like every conversation ends with the more expensive option.

Licensed, Insured, and Accountable

Every technician we send is licensed through the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners, the single statewide authority that licenses plumbers in Texas (Texas doesn't run city-by-city licensing the way some states do). That license means real accountability: a technician working under a valid license has a board they answer to if something goes wrong, which isn't the case for unlicensed handyman work that sometimes gets offered around older neighborhoods at a lower price.

We're also insured, which protects you if something unexpected happens during a job, and we pull permits for any work that requires one under Pasadena's building code, sewer line replacement and gas line installation being the most common examples. None of this is optional or an upsell; it's the baseline of doing plumbing work correctly in this state, and we don't cut corners on it to shave a few dollars off a quote.

A Straightforward Way To Reach Us

We don't run an online booking form or a chatbot. Every job starts with an actual phone conversation, where we can ask the right follow-up questions, get a real sense of what's happening, and give you an honest read on urgency before anyone's dispatched. If you'd rather email first, that works too, but for anything urgent, calling gets you a faster answer, since it lets us start figuring out the right technician and parts while we're still talking.
