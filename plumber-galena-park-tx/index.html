Plumbing Service in Galena Park, TX

Galena Park is one of the smaller, older cities in this part of Harris County -- just under 5 square miles, a population around 10,700, and a history that goes back to the Galena Signal Oil Company, which built the area's first refinery and gave the city its name. It sits right against the Houston Ship Channel, closer to the Turning Basin than any of the other towns we cover.

Housing here is some of the oldest in our service area -- wood-frame houses and 1950s-60s brick tract homes dominate the residential streets, which means original plumbing is more the rule than the exception in a lot of Galena Park calls.

About Galena Park

Galena Park is served by its own Galena Park Independent School District, with Galena Park High School anchoring the community, and sits within easy reach of San Jacinto College's North Campus. The city's southern boundary runs along the Houston Ship Channel, with Interstate 610 to the west connecting it into the wider Houston grid.

The city's proximity to downtown Houston's University of Houston campus and San Jacinto College's North Campus makes it a practical, affordable option for a lot of families working in the surrounding petrochemical corridor, and the tree-lined residential streets give it a quieter feel than its industrial neighbors along the Ship Channel might suggest. Many of these streets still carry the original grid layout from the city's early railroad-era development.

Why It's Different Here

Galena Park's housing stock skews older than almost anywhere else we serve -- much of it built in the 1950s and 60s on the original tract layout -- so galvanized supply lines, aging cast iron drains, and water heaters well past their rated life are a bigger share of what we see on a typical Galena Park call than in newer parts of the metro.

Given how much of the housing stock here dates to the 1950s and 60s, repiping and sewer line repair come up more often in Galena Park than in newer parts of the metro. We're also a short run from South Houston and Pasadena itself.

Galena Park's 77547 borders Houston's 77012, 77015, and 77029 along the Ship Channel side and Pasadena's 77502 and 77506 to the south, and repiping calls from any of those bordering ZIPs run on the same schedule as a call from inside Galena Park proper.
