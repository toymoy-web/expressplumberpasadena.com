Shower & Bathtub Installation in Pasadena, TX

Whether it's a leaking shower valve, a tub that needs to become a walk-in shower, or a full bathroom remodel rough-in, we handle the plumbing side: valve replacement, drain relocation, and supply line work behind the wall.

For tub-to-shower conversions specifically, we coordinate the plumbing changes, moving the drain and supply lines to the new fixture's footprint, so the finish work goes in clean.

If the existing rough-in doesn't support the fixture you want, we'll say so during the estimate rather than discover it mid-installation.

Signs You Need This

When To Call For Shower & Bathtub Installation

  • ✓A shower valve that's leaking behind the wall or won't hold temperature
  • ✓Wanting to convert a bathtub to a walk-in shower
  • ✓A tub or shower drain that's slow or clogged at the fixture level
  • ✓Planning a bathroom remodel that needs the plumbing rough-in redone
  • ✓A shower valve that takes noticeably longer to reach temperature than it used to
In Pasadena, TX

A number of Pasadena homeowners in the area's older housing stock are converting a rarely-used tub to a walk-in shower as part of aging-in-place remodels. That conversion means relocating the drain and supply lines to match the new fixture footprint, which we handle as part of the plumbing rough-in.

Because many of these homes still have original cast iron drain stubs, matching a new fixture to the existing rough-in sometimes needs an adapter or a short section of new drain line, which we scope during the estimate rather than discovering mid-project.

This kind of remodel work often turns up other issues worth addressing at the same time, like valve or fixture repairs elsewhere in the bathroom, or a toilet replacement if you're updating the whole room.

Shower and tub installs usually happen during a remodel, and whether the house is near Burke/Crenshaw Park or out toward Pasadena Highlands, we rough in the valve and drain before anyone closes up the wall.

Our Process

How We Handle It

1

Assess the current plumbing

We check existing valve, drain, and supply line positions against the new fixture's requirements.

2

Relocate or replace as needed

Drain and supply lines are moved or upgraded to match the new footprint.

3

Pressure-test before finish work

Everything is tested and confirmed leak-free before tile or surround installation begins.
