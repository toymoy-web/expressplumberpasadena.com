Burst Pipe Repair

Burst Pipe Repair in Pasadena, TX

A burst pipe is a shut-the-water-off-now situation. If you can locate your home's main shutoff valve, close it before calling -- it limits the damage while we're on the way.

Once on site, we isolate the affected line, replace the damaged section, and pressure-test the repair before restoring water to the house, so you're not left wondering if it will hold.

Once the immediate repair is done, we'll also flag any other visibly aging pipe nearby so a second failure doesn't catch you off guard next season.

Signs You Need This

When To Call For Burst Pipe Repair

  • ✓Water actively spraying or pooling from a visible pipe
  • ✓A sudden, dramatic drop in water pressure throughout the house
  • ✓Water stains spreading quickly across a ceiling or wall
  • ✓The sound of rushing water inside a wall cavity
  • ✓A sudden spray or spike in water pressure right before the failure
In Pasadena, TX

Pasadena doesn't see hard freezes often, but the rare winter cold snap that does reach the Gulf Coast catches exposed hose bibs and uninsulated exterior pipe runs off guard, since most homes here aren't built with the pipe insulation standard in colder climates.

Older galvanized supply lines common in this area's original neighborhoods are also more brittle at pipe joints than modern copper or PEX, which is why a sudden temperature swing or a surge in water pressure is more likely to cause a burst here than in a newer subdivision.

Once the emergency is handled, it's worth having a full leak detection sweep done, since a freeze or pressure spike severe enough to burst one pipe can stress others nearby. If the home's original lines are the underlying issue, that conversation sometimes leads to a full repipe.

A burst pipe does not wait for business hours, and our emergency line covers everything from the blocks near the Pasadena Convention Center to the Genoa and Red Bluff areas on the south side. Shut off the main first, then call.

Our Process

How We Handle It

1

Isolate the line

We shut off the affected section, not the whole house, when the layout allows it.

2

Replace the damaged section

The burst or split section is cut out and replaced with matching or upgraded pipe.

3

Pressure-test before restoring

Water stays off that line until the repair is pressure-tested and confirmed to hold.
