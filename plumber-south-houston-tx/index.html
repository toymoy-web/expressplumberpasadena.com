Plumbing Service in South Houston, TX

South Houston is its own incorporated city -- not a Houston neighborhood despite the name -- sitting directly against Pasadena's western edge in southeast Harris County. It's one of the smallest and most densely built cities in the area at just over 3 square miles, incorporated back in 1913, which means a lot of the housing stock here is old enough to still be running original plumbing that's never been touched.

We dispatch to South Houston on the same schedule as Pasadena itself -- it's a five-minute run for our trucks, not a separate service call with separate wait times.

About South Houston

South Houston is served by the Pasadena Independent School District and sits within a few minutes of San Jacinto College's South Campus. The city runs along the I-45 corridor with Beltway 8 close by, and most of the housing here is single-family homes on established lots dating to the mid-20th century -- a similar age profile to Pasadena's own older neighborhoods, which is part of why we see the same plumbing issues show up in both cities.

South Houston residents typically head to Pasadena Town Square Mall or the H-E-B a few minutes away for shopping, and Crenshaw Park serves as the area's main green space. The city's compact footprint means most calls here are a short hop from our Pasadena dispatch point, and it's common for a South Houston service address to be closer to our base than some parts of Pasadena itself. That proximity also means parts and equipment sourced for a South Houston job usually arrive on the same trip.

Why It's Different Here

Because South Houston incorporated in 1913 and built out steadily through the mid-1900s, a meaningful share of homes here are old enough to have original galvanized supply lines or aging cast iron drains -- the same issue we see across Pasadena's historic core, just concentrated in an even smaller, denser footprint.

Given how many South Houston homes still run original plumbing, repiping and leak detection are two of our most common calls here, alongside routine drain cleaning. We also dispatch just as quickly to neighboring Galena Park and, of course, back into Pasadena itself.

South Houston's 77587 borders Houston's 77017 and 77034 to the south and Pasadena's own 77502 and 77504 to the north and east, and we run water heater repair and drain cleaning calls across that whole stretch on the same route, not just within the 77587 boundary itself.
