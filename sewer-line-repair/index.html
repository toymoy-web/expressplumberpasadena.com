Sewer Line Repair

Sewer Line Repair in Pasadena, TX

A cracked, offset, or root-damaged sewer line usually shows up first as a recurring backup, a slow-draining main line, or a wet, low spot in the yard above where the pipe runs.

Not every damaged sewer line needs full replacement. A camera inspection locates the exact damage first, and a localized crack or a single offset joint is often fixable with a spot repair or trenchless lining rather than digging up the whole line.

If the camera shows more than one damaged section, we'll say so plainly rather than quote a spot repair that's likely to need a second visit soon after.

Signs You Need This

When To Call For Sewer Line Repair

  • ✓Sewage backing up into a tub, shower, or floor drain
  • ✓A consistently soggy or unusually green patch of lawn above the line
  • ✓Gurgling from toilets when the washing machine drains
  • ✓A camera inspection showing a crack, offset joint, or root intrusion
  • ✓A sewer smell noticeable in the yard even without visible standing water
In Pasadena, TX

The Gulf Coast clay soil under most Pasadena lots expands and contracts with the season, and that movement is a common cause of offset joints and hairline cracks in older sewer laterals: a spot repair at the damaged joint is often enough without replacing the whole line.

Many of these laterals also date to the same mid-20th-century construction wave as the homes above them, which means the pipe material itself, clay or early cast iron, is often as much a factor in the failure as the soil movement around it.

When the damage is more extensive than a single spot repair can fix, full lateral replacement becomes the honest recommendation. Either way, a stubborn clog beforehand is often what prompted the camera inspection in the first place.

From the older blocks around Shaver Street to the neighborhoods along Allen Genoa Road, a sewer backup usually starts with a camera inspection, so we can show you the damage on a screen before anyone digs.

24/7 Emergency Response

Emergency Sewer Backup Response in Pasadena, TX

Sewage coming up through a floor drain, a toilet that gurgles when the washer runs, or wastewater in the yard are all urgent. Our 24/7 line dispatches sewer line calls at any hour, and a camera inspection finds out whether the problem is a simple blockage or a damaged pipe.

Sewage is a health hazard, so the steps below focus on keeping people and belongings clear until we arrive.

  • ✓Stop all water use in the house, including flushing, showers, and laundry
  • ✓Keep children and pets away from any water that has backed up, and avoid touching it
  • ✓Open windows if there is an odor, and keep fans pointed away from the affected area
  • ✓Take photos of the affected area for your records, then call our 24/7 line
