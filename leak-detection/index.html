Leak Detection

Leak Detection in Pasadena, TX

A water bill that's climbed with no obvious cause, a damp patch on a wall or ceiling, or the sound of running water when nothing's turned on all point to a leak you can't see -- and tearing open drywall or slab to go looking for it is the expensive, last-resort way to find one.

We use acoustic listening equipment and electronic leak-tracing tools to pinpoint a hidden leak's location before any repair work starts, which is what actually keeps the fix small instead of turning into a demolition project.

If tracing points to something outside what we handle -- a leak on the utility's side of the meter, for instance -- we'll tell you that directly instead of running up a bill chasing it.

Signs You Need This

When To Call For Leak Detection

  • ✓A water bill that's risen with no change in usage
  • ✓The water meter still turning with every fixture in the house off
  • ✓A damp spot, warm spot, or discoloration on a wall, ceiling, or floor
  • ✓The sound of running water with no visible source
  • ✓A meter that keeps spinning even after every fixture is confirmed off
In Pasadena, TX

Slab foundations are the norm across most of Pasadena, and that means a supply line leak often develops underneath the concrete where it's completely invisible until the water finds its way up through a floor seam -- electronic detection finds it before that happens.

The same clay-soil movement that stresses sewer laterals in this area also flexes the slab itself slightly over time, which is one more reason a supply line leak under the floor is a real, recurring call here rather than a rare event.

If tracing points to something beneath the foundation specifically, that becomes a slab leak case rather than a standard fixture leak. A leak found in the yard between the meter and the house instead usually means a water line repair.
