Garbage Disposal Repair & Installation

Garbage Disposal Repair & Installation in Pasadena, TX

A disposal that hums but won't turn, jams repeatedly, or leaks from the bottom is usually either a stuck impeller, a worn seal, or a motor at the end of its life, and we diagnose which before recommending a repair or a new unit.

New installs and swaps to a higher-horsepower unit are quick, same-visit jobs in most kitchens, including reconnecting the dishwasher drain line correctly, which is a common miss on DIY installs.

If the unit's simply past a reasonable repair point, we'll say so plainly rather than keep billing for parts on something close to end of life.

Signs You Need This

When To Call For Garbage Disposal Repair & Installation

  • ✓A humming sound but the blades don't spin
  • ✓Water leaking from the bottom of the disposal unit
  • ✓Frequent jamming even with normal food waste
  • ✓Unusually loud grinding or rattling noise during operation
  • ✓A disposal that trips the circuit breaker when switched on
In Pasadena, TX

Older Pasadena homes near the historic core often still have the original under-sink plumbing from before disposals were standard, which means a new install sometimes needs a minor drain line adjustment to fit properly: something we check during the estimate, not after the unit's already mounted.

Because those older drain configurations vary house to house, we've found it's worth confirming trap and vent placement before quoting a swap, rather than assuming every kitchen in the area matches a standard modern layout.

A disposal replacement is a good time to check nearby faucet and sink hardware too, since it's already open under the counter. For commercial kitchens, see our commercial plumbing page instead.

Disposal jams and leaks are routine calls from Pasadena kitchens, and a unit that hums but will not turn is usually a quick fix. Food-service businesses are a separate category that we treat as commercial work.

24/7 Emergency Response

Emergency Disposal Jam, Leak, and Backup Repair in Pasadena, TX

A disposal that hums but will not spin, a leak pooling in the cabinet, or a kitchen sink that backs up the moment the unit runs can stop a household cold. Our 24/7 line dispatches a licensed plumber at any hour, and we quote a flat price before starting.

A few safe steps keep the problem from getting worse while you wait.

  • ✓Switch the disposal off at the wall and never put a hand inside the chamber
  • ✓Turn off the power at the breaker or unplug the unit if it is leaking or sparking
  • ✓If water is leaking, close the supply valves under the sink and set a bucket below
  • ✓Stop running the dishwasher and the sink, then call our 24/7 line
Our Process

How We Handle It

1

Test the unit

We check the reset button, impeller movement, and seals before deciding repair or replace.

2

Clear or swap the unit

A jam gets cleared by hand. A failed motor or seal means installing a new disposal.

3

Reconnect and test

Dishwasher drain line reconnected correctly, then run under water to confirm no leaks.
