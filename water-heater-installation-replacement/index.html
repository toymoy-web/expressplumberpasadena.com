Water Heater Installation & Replacement in Pasadena, TX

When a water heater is beyond repair or you're ready to upgrade, we handle the full replacement: sizing the new unit to your household, removing and hauling away the old tank, and installing to current code, including the expansion tank and drain pan requirements many older installs are missing.

Same-day replacement is available for calls before 2pm on most standard tank swaps. Tankless conversions take longer since gas line and venting work is usually involved -- we'll scope that during the estimate, not after we've started.

We'll also flag it honestly if a second issue is developing on the same unit, rather than fix the immediate problem and let a related one turn into a callback next month.

Signs You Need This

When To Call For Water Heater Installation & Replacement

  • ✓The tank is leaking from the body itself, not a fitting
  • ✓The unit is 10+ years old and now needs a second or third repair
  • ✓You're constantly running out of hot water for your household size
  • ✓You're remodeling and want to switch from tank to tankless
  • ✓A leak visible right at the T&P relief valve fitting
In Pasadena, TX

Homes in Pasadena's older neighborhoods near the historic core were mostly built before expansion tanks and drain pans were standard code requirements. When we replace a unit in one of these houses, bringing the install up to current code is part of the job, not an upsell.

Because the municipal water here runs hard, we also flag when a new tank installation makes sense to pair with a softener -- it's the single biggest factor in how long the new unit will actually last before its next service call.

If your household is considering a different type of unit entirely, our tankless water heater page walks through whether that switch makes sense. For households in Baytown with a mix of older and newer construction, the right choice often depends on which era of the house you're in.

We install replacement units for homes near Strawberry Park, Burke/Crenshaw Park, and Pasadena Highlands, and the old tank leaves on the same visit. Pasadena covers roughly 44 square miles south of the Ship Channel, so give us your cross streets when you call and we will quote a realistic arrival window.

Our Process

How We Handle It

1

Size the replacement

We measure household demand -- fixtures, bathrooms, simultaneous use -- against tank or tankless options.

2

Remove and haul away

The old unit comes out and off the property; nothing left in your garage or closet.

3

Install to current code

Expansion tank, drain pan, and venting brought up to code as part of the install, then leak-tested.
