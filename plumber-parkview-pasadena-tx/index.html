Plumbing Service in the Parkview Area

Parkview is not one neighborhood but a cluster of three. Wikipedia lists Parkview Estates, Parkview Manor, and Parkview South as separate Pasadena neighborhoods, and neighborhood profiles place them in ZIP 77502. This page covers all three because homeowners use the Parkview name for the whole area.

The City of Pasadena Parks Guide lists Parkview Park at 2400 Burke Rd, with Burke/Crenshaw Park at 4950 Burke Rd and Universal/All-Access Park at 5200 Burke Rd along the same road.

About The Area

Profiles describe Parkview Manor as mainly single-family homes and townhomes, mostly owner-occupied, with many built between 1940 and 1969. Parkview Estates is described as single-family homes and mobile homes from a similar span. These are listing-site descriptions, not surveys.

Parkview South is described as a more mixed owner and renter area near Fairmont Park and Crenshaw Park, and Pasadena ISD serves the area. Boundaries between the three are not published, so we refer to the area by its anchor, Burke Road.

What Sets It Apart

Long-Owned Homes And Remodel Work

Where owners stay for decades, plumbing work tends to be about upkeep and upgrades rather than emergencies alone. Replacing a worn fixture, swapping an old water heater before it fails, or opening a bathroom for a remodel are all common reasons to call.

We keep the advice practical. A faucet repair, a new toilet installation, or a larger repiping project each gets a written explanation of what is needed and what it costs before work starts.

If water is running where it should not be, close the main valve first and call. The City of Pasadena water meter FAQ says customers are responsible for water service lines from the meter to the home, so that stretch is the owner's repair.

For a recurring backup, a camera inspection can show whether the problem is a buildup in the drain or something further along. Our drain cleaning crew can run the inspection and explain what it found.
