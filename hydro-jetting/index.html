Hydro Jetting

Hydro Jetting in Pasadena, TX

Hydro jetting uses a high-pressure water stream, run through a specialized nozzle down the line, to scour the full interior diameter of a pipe clean: grease buildup, mineral scale, and root intrusion all get cut away, not just punched through.

It's the right call when a line has been snaked before and clogs again within weeks, or when a camera inspection shows buildup coating the pipe walls rather than a single blockage sitting in one spot.

We'll also tell you honestly when a line's condition means jetting isn't the right first step, rather than run the equipment and charge for it regardless of outcome.

Signs You Need This

When To Call For Hydro Jetting

  • ✓A line that was recently snaked but has already clogged again
  • ✓Multiple drains on the same line backing up together
  • ✓A camera inspection showing grease or scale coating the pipe interior
  • ✓Tree root intrusion identified in a sewer or yard line
  • ✓A gurgling floor drain in a garage or utility room
In Pasadena, TX

Mature trees are common in Pasadena's older residential streets, and their root systems reach for the moisture around older clay or cast iron sewer laterals: hydro jetting clears root intrusion from the inside of the line without excavating the yard first.

The same aging cast iron and clay lines common in this area's original neighborhoods also accumulate mineral scale from the local water supply over decades, which is a second, separate reason jetting tends to be recommended here more often than in newer subdivisions with PVC lines.

If jetting turns up a genuinely damaged section rather than just buildup, that becomes a sewer line repair conversation instead. Many Galena Park calls start as a jetting request and end up needing that follow-up once the camera footage comes back.

Restaurants along Fairmont Parkway and homes on older main lines both end up needing jetting eventually, because grease and mineral scale build up faster than a cable can clear them. Jetting scours the pipe wall instead of just punching a hole through the clog.

Emergency Hydro Jetting for Backed-Up Lines in Pasadena, TX

When a sewer or drain line is completely blocked, a standard cable sometimes clears a small hole and the clog returns in days. Hydro jetting scours the full width of the pipe, which is why it is the right call for grease buildup, heavy sediment, and roots that keep coming back.

Our 24/7 line dispatches jetting at any hour. We camera the line first to confirm it can take the pressure, then quote a flat price before any work starts.
