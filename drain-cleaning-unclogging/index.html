Drain Cleaning & Unclogging

Drain Cleaning & Unclogging in Pasadena, TX

A slow or fully clogged drain is almost always grease, hair, soap scum, or a foreign object sitting somewhere in the line -- and a chemical drain cleaner from the store usually just pushes the clog further down without clearing it.

We use a mechanical drain snake or cable machine sized to the line -- kitchen, bathroom, or main -- to physically break up and clear the blockage, then run water to confirm flow is fully restored before we leave.

For a clog that's cleared repeatedly in the same spot, we'll recommend the camera look upfront rather than snake it a third time and have you call again in a few weeks.

Signs You Need This

When To Call For Drain Cleaning & Unclogging

  • ✓Water draining slowly in a sink, tub, or shower
  • ✓Gurgling sounds from a drain when another fixture is used
  • ✓A drain that's fully backed up and standing with water
  • ✓Recurring clogs in the same drain every few months
  • ✓Water backing up into a different fixture than the one being used
In Pasadena, TX

Older homes in Pasadena's original neighborhoods often still run cast iron drain lines, which corrode and roughen on the inside over decades -- that rough interior surface catches debris far more easily than a smooth PVC line, which is why the same drain can clog repeatedly even with normal use.

Combined with the area's mature tree canopy, those older cast iron lines are also more exposed to root intrusion at aging joints, so a drain that clogs on a predictable schedule is worth a camera look rather than another routine snaking.

For a line that keeps clogging no matter how many times it's snaked, hydro jetting is usually the next step, and a sewer line repair visit may be worth considering if a camera inspection shows real damage rather than just buildup.

Drain calls arrive from homes near Vince Bayou Park, Satsuma Park, and the streets off Southmore Avenue, where older lines and mature trees make root intrusion a real possibility. A camera look tells us whether we are dealing with grease, scale, or roots before we choose a method.
