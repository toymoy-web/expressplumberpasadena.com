Faucet & Sink Repair

Faucet & Sink Repair in Pasadena, TX

A dripping faucet is almost always a worn cartridge, O-ring, or washer inside the fixture: a small, inexpensive part that's cheaper to replace than the water it wastes over a year.

We repair or replace kitchen and bathroom faucets, fix leaking supply lines under sinks, and handle full sink fixture replacements when a unit is too corroded or dated to keep repairing.

If the supply valve under the sink is also corroded, we'll mention it during the same visit rather than have it fail on its own a few months later.

Signs You Need This

When To Call For Faucet & Sink Repair

  • ✓A faucet that drips steadily even when fully shut off
  • ✓Low water pressure from a single fixture, not the whole house
  • ✓Water pooling under the sink from a supply line or drain fitting
  • ✓A faucet handle that's loose, stiff, or hard to turn
  • ✓A faucet handle that requires more force than it used to, to turn on or off
In Pasadena, TX

Mineral deposits can build up inside faucet cartridges and aerators wherever the water carries dissolved minerals, which is a common reason a faucet that's only a few years old already feels stiff or restricted.

Older homes near Pasadena's historic core also tend to have original supply valves under the sink, which can seize up from mineral buildup the same way: something we check whenever we're already under a sink for a faucet repair.

A slow kitchen drain often gets mentioned in the same call as a faucet repair. See our drain cleaning page if that's part of what's going on. We also handle garbage disposal issues during the same kitchen visit.

Fixtures that have been in place for years are the first to show mineral scale, and faucet and sink repairs fill our schedule in neighborhoods from Pasadena Highlands to Golden Acres.

24/7 Emergency Response

Emergency Faucet, Valve, and Under-Sink Leak Repair in Pasadena, TX

A supply valve that has cracked, a connector spraying under the sink, or a faucet that will not shut off can flood a kitchen or bathroom quickly. Our 24/7 line sends a licensed plumber at any hour, and we quote a flat price before work begins.

The fastest fix is usually the shutoff right under the fixture, so start there.

  • ✓Turn the small valve under the sink clockwise to cut off that fixture
  • ✓If the valve itself is leaking or will not turn, close the main shutoff for the house
  • ✓Set a bucket under the leak and lay towels to protect cabinets and flooring
  • ✓Do not overtighten a leaking fitting, since cracked parts make the repair larger, and call our 24/7 line
Fixture Calls

Fixture Leaks and Valve Failures We Repair

Fixture problems are small until they are not. These are the calls we dispatch most often, including after hours.

Under-sink supply valves and connectors

Corroded stops and braided connectors fail without warning. We replace the failing part and check the neighbors.

Shower valves and tub faucets

A cartridge or valve that leaks behind the wall, or a tub faucet that will not shut off, needs a repair that matches your fixture.

Laundry and utility sinks

Washing machine valves, utility sink faucets, and laundry connections all leak at the fittings first, so we start there.

Outdoor spigots

A spigot that drips after winter or a hose bib leaking inside the wall can be repaired, and we can add new outdoor faucets when needed.

Our Process

How We Handle It

1

Identify the failed part

Cartridge, O-ring, washer, or supply line: pinpointed before any parts are ordered.

2

Repair or replace the fixture

A worn part is swapped, or the full faucet is replaced if it's too corroded to service.

3

Check for leaks under load

We run the fixture at full flow and check every connection before calling it done.
