Sump Pump Repair & Installation in Pasadena, TX

Most Pasadena homes sit on slab foundations rather than over basements, so a sump pump is a less common fixture here than in colder parts of the country.

Where one does exist, it is usually doing a very specific job: keeping a crawlspace, a below-grade utility room, a garage drainage pit, or the low corner of a lot from filling with water after heavy rain.

When that pump fails during a storm, it tends to fail at the worst possible time.

Pasadena's flat terrain and slow-draining clay give rainwater few places to go, and the city has installed stormwater pump stations to keep major roads open during heavy rain. Harris County Flood Control District meteorologist Jeff Lindner has said that about 60 percent of the homes that flood in the county are outside the mapped floodplain, so a pump that works matters even for homes that never thought of themselves as flood-prone.

We repair and replace residential and light commercial sump pumps, both pedestal and submersible, and we install battery backups so the pump keeps running when a Gulf storm takes the power out. If you do not actually need a sump pump, we will say so and point you toward a drainage fix that suits the lot.

Signs You Need This

When To Call For Sump Pump Repair & Installation

  • ✓A pump that runs constantly or cycles on and off every few seconds
  • ✓A pump that hums but does not move water, or never turns on at all
  • ✓Water standing in the pit, crawlspace, or low utility room after rain
  • ✓A grinding, rattling, or burning-smell noise from the pump motor
  • ✓A pump more than seven to ten years old with no battery backup
In Pasadena, TX

Heavy rain has flooded parts of Pasadena more than once. A May 2020 storm put water on Red Bluff Road, and Hurricane Harvey in 2017 flooded enough Pasadena homes that the local Salvation Army opened its gym as a shelter. Properties near Vince Bayou, Little Vince Bayou, and Armand Bayou are where drainage questions come up most.

Power loss is the other local factor. Gulf storms knock out electricity right when a pump is working hardest, which is why a battery backup is worth discussing with any pump installation in this area.

If water is coming up through a floor drain instead of collecting in a pit, that points to a sewer line problem rather than a pump problem. Properties that also need protection against reverse flow should see our backflow page, and an unexplained wet crawlspace is often worth a leak detection visit before anyone installs a pump.

Sump pump calls cluster near the bayous and low drainage areas around Vince Bayou and the Genoa corridor, and after storms like Harvey, when pumps that sat idle for months are suddenly asked to run for days.

24/7 Emergency Response

Emergency Sump Pump Failure Response in Pasadena, TX

A sump pump that stops when the pit is filling is a race against rising water. Heavy rain along the Gulf Coast can overwhelm a pump in hours, and a failed float switch, a dead motor, or a power loss all look the same from the outside.

Our 24/7 line takes sump pump emergencies at any hour, including during storms. We can repair the pump, replace it, or add a battery backup so the next outage does not repeat the problem.

  • ✓Check that the pump is plugged in and that the breaker or GFCI outlet has not tripped
  • ✓Do not reach into the pit while the pump is plugged in
  • ✓Move belongings off the floor in any area where water is rising
  • ✓Call our 24/7 line and tell us whether the pump is silent, humming, or running without moving water
Our Process

How We Handle It

1

Test and diagnose

We check the float, the pump motor, the check valve, and the discharge line to find the real failure.

2

Repair or replace

We quote a flat price for a repair or a new pump sized to your pit before any work starts.

3

Test under load

We run water through the system and confirm the pump clears the pit and the discharge drains away from the house.
